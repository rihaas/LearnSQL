5 Habits of Top 1% SQL Candidates

Live Screen Rule
  1. 1 Clarify the Grain First: Before typing SELECT, ask: "Can a customer have multiple orders on the same timestamp? Are there NULLs or negative refunds?"
  2. 2 Modular CTEs Over Nested Subqueries: Name CTEs by business step (WITH Deduplicated_Invoices AS (...)) so the interviewer follows your logic effortlessly.
  3. 3 Defensive Division & NULLs: Always wrap denominators in NULLIF(denom, 0) and use NOT EXISTS instead of NOT IN.
  4. 4 Sargable Predicates: Never wrap an indexed date column in YEAR() or CAST() inside WHERE.
  5. 5 State the Complexity: Proactively mention if your query triggers a sort, hash join, or window spill on 100M+ rows.
